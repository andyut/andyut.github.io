Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

YA Consulting ("we") operates a private app that connects only to our own Pinterest account to create, schedule and report on our own Pins.

Data we access

The app accesses only our own Pinterest account data (boards, Pins and basic analytics) through the official Pinterest API. We do not access, collect or store data from other Pinterest users.

Use of data

Data is used solely to publish and manage our own content. We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored securely and are never shared publicly. You can revoke access at any time from Pinterest settings.
